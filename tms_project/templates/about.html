About Training Management System

Training Management System (TMS) is a smart web-based platform used to manage and organize training activities efficiently.

  • ✔ Manage training programs and schedules easily
  • ✔ Track student attendance automatically
  • ✔ Store student and trainer details
  • ✔ Generate certificates after course completion
  • ✔ Provide role-based login (Admin, Trainer, Student)
  • ✔ Analyze performance using reports and analytics
  • ✔ Send notifications via email or SMS
  • ✔ Reduce manual work and improve efficiency

This system helps organizations save time, improve productivity, and manage training processes in a simple and effective way.
