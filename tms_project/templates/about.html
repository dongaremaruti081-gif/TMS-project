About TMS

📚 About Training Management System

Training Management System (TMS) is a smart web-based platform used to manage and organize training activities efficiently. It provides comprehensive solutions for organizations to streamline their training processes and enhance employee development.

✨ Key Features & Benefits

  • Manage training programs and schedules easily
  • Track student attendance automatically
  • Store student and trainer details securely
  • Generate certificates after course completion
  • Provide role-based login (Admin, Trainer, Student)
  • Analyze performance using reports and analytics
  • Send notifications via email or SMS
  • Reduce manual work and improve efficiency

This system helps organizations save time, improve productivity, and manage training processes in a simple and effective way. With our user-friendly interface and powerful features, TMS is the perfect solution for modern training management needs.
