Frequently Asked Questions ❓

Find answers to the most common questions about our Training Management System.

What is TMS?
TMS (Training Management System) is a platform designed to manage training programs, employees, attendance, certificates, and performance efficiently.
Who can use TMS?
Organizations, educational institutes, corporate trainers, HR teams, and training centers can use TMS.
Does TMS provide certificate generation?
Yes, TMS automatically generates professional certificates after successful course completion.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. TMS uses secure authentication, encrypted storage, and advanced data protection mechanisms.
Can I track trainee attendance?
Yes, TMS offers automated attendance tracking and detailed attendance reports.
How do I get started?
Simply register an account, log in, and begin creating training programs right away.
